GANGASHREE ROOTS PRIVATE LIMITED

Engineering excellence.
Refreshing experiences.

GangaShree Roots Private Limited is a packaged drinking water and beverages company behind the Revenger brand. We are focused on building customer confidence through quality-oriented processes, operational discipline and trusted partnerships.

Our portfolio brings together packaged drinking water, soda, jeera soda and lemon soda. We aim to grow responsibly, strengthening our relationships with retailers, distributors, institutional customers and business partners.

Our vision

To become a trusted name in packaged drinking water and beverages through consistent quality, innovation, efficient operations and sustainable business development.

Our mission

To deliver reliable, refreshing beverage products while improving operational standards, strengthening customer relationships and expanding our market presence.
